Getting started

Find your dealership. That’s the sign-up.

Tap your state, start typing your name, and pick it from the Texas licence list. We send a code to the phone or email on your licence, so nobody can set up your dealership but you.

  • Your licence number, city and county come from the state
  • Your logo and colour go on every document
  • Tell us what you use today, and we’ll plan the switch with you
Paperwork

A bill of sale you’d be proud to hand over.

Your name, address and GDN at the top. The vehicle, the odometer statement and the terms in clean ruled boxes. The price to the cent, the Texas doc-fee notice, and signature lines for both of you.

  • Bill of sale and Title Application (130-U) fields filled from the deal
  • Rebuilt and salvage titles get their own disclosures
  • Print it, or sign it on the screen
Handle a sale

One question per screen. The answer is the next tap.

Which car. What the odometer says. Who’s buying, read back so they can check it. How they’re paying. The Desk works out tax, title, registration and your doc fee as you go, and knows which papers this deal needs.

  • Cash, buy here pay here or bank financing
  • Out-the-door or plus tax and fees
  • Nobody has to be shown how to use it
Signing

Signed at the desk. Filed in order.

Hand the buyer the screen. They read each page, sign with a finger, and hand it back. You see what’s signed, what’s filed and what still needs ink, in one list.

  • Every page shows what it means in one plain sentence
  • Forms that need ink are printed for ink, never signed on a screen
  • Print the whole packet when you need paper
