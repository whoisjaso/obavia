Questions.

Can we buy today?

Not yet. Obavia is in development and the waitlist is free. Core is planned at $3,000 a month; everything else is proposed and will be confirmed before any purchase.

Does it replace our sales method?

No. It works inside your funnel, your SOPs and your language, and carries each client’s context from the first call to collected payment.

Do owners need a seat?

The proposal gives owners and managers read access without taking an active selling seat.

What’s outside the standard scope?

AI outbound voice, SMS and carrier fees, data enrichment, premium CRM subscriptions, bespoke migration and custom integrations are scoped separately.
