# Obavia — Report 2: Marketing and Go-to-Market
Deep research synthesis, 2026-09-19. Reference data for Claude and Codex planning sessions. Every claim used in an ad must exist in `docs/CLAIMS_REGISTRY.md` first.

## 1. Positioning
- **Company line**: Obavia helps people and dealerships complete vehicle transactions with less repeated work, clearer decisions, and visible accountability.
- **Buyer line**: *Know what you can actually get before you walk onto a lot.* (ES: *Sabe qué te alcanza de verdad antes de pisar un lote.*)
- **Dealer line**: *You already have enough to do. Obavia keeps the deal moving.* (ES: *Ya tienes bastante que hacer. Obavia mantiene la venta en marcha.*)
- **Rules that never change**: never "dealers are shady"; never "nobody is buying cars" (the dry-lot theme is a question, not a claim); never "approved", "prequalified", "guaranteed", bare "verified", or "no hidden fees" as a platform promise.

## 2. Ideal customer profiles and sizing
**Dealer ICP**: Texas independent dealer, GDN-licensed, 1–5 people, 10–80 units/month, often BHPH, owner does sales + docs + auction + recon, EN/ES, already pays $55–$300/mo for a DMS and $0–$2,500/mo for listings, phone-first.
- Sizing [V/I]: ~117k licensed independent locations nationally, ~53k active; Texas ≈ 10% of active independents; Harris County ≈ 2,800 dealers (~395 franchise). Houston metro independents: 1,500–2,400 [I]. TxDMV publishes a statewide dealer list; TIADA can confirm counts.

**Buyer ICP**: Houston-area used-car shopper, 22–45, phone-first, budget-first ($300–$600/mo), credit fair-to-building, often first-time or Spanish-speaking household, has been burned by fees or a listing that wasn't the car.
- Facts [V]: 74% of Hispanic adults are bilingual; 59% prefer English ads with culturally relevant content, so creative must be bilingual, not translated. YouTube reaches 86% of Hispanic adults; TikTok engagement is higher; Spanish-language auto creative shows a measurable lift. One Texas dealership saw +25% repeat Hispanic buyers after Spanish content and bilingual staff.
- Houston: 82,861 used vehicles listed within 100 miles, mean asking $29,015 (Autotrader, Sept 2026) [V].

## 3. Meta rules that bind every campaign
- **Special Ad Category: Credit.** Any ad that mentions financing, credit, approval, loans, or rates is a credit ad: no ZIP targeting (state or 15-mile radius minimum), no age/gender/interest targeting, no lookalikes, customer-list custom audiences need certification (from March 2025), wealth-based audiences banned (Sept 2025) [V].
- **Consequence**: buyer ads must speak of estimates, budgets, and transparency, never credit. That is also what the never-list requires. Dealer ads (B2B) are not credit ads as long as they sell software, not financing.
- **Benchmarks [V]**: automotive CPM $18–$35; Reels 38% lower CPM, 86% lower CPL than static ($1.20 vs $8.50 in one dataset); dealer CPL typical $28–$50; auto ROAS ≈ 2.5x. Treat as ranges, not targets.
- **Customer lists**: dealer lists (TIADA, auction contacts, Triple J network) are B2B and usable for custom and lookalike audiences. Buyer lists are not, once the ad touches credit.

## 4. Campaign concepts (EN / ES)
Each: audience · hook · format · guardrail · what we measure. Metric that matters is downstream (interviews, paid continuation, inquiries that become conversations), not signups.

**D1 — "Does your lot feel slower than it used to?"** (dealer, question form only)
- ES: *¿Sientes tu lote más lento que antes?*
- Owner-operators 30–60, Texas metros; 15-second vertical video of a real lot at 2 pm.
- Guardrail: question only; never state that nobody is buying.
- Measure: interview requests per $300.

**D2 — "You are the whole dealership."**
- ES: *Tú eres todo el concesionario.*
- Copy: mechanic, doc clerk, buyer, salesperson, collector. One inbox. One deal file. Today mode.
- Format: carousel of the Today screen, EN and ES panels.
- Measure: founding applications.

**D3 — "Texts that sound like you, and know when to stop."**
- ES: *Mensajes que suenan como tú, y saben cuándo parar.*
- Show a real thread: dealer-voice draft, consent line, STOP honored, the AI refusing to quote a price and handing to the human.
- Guardrail: show disclosure in the message; never claim the AI closes deals.
- Measure: demo requests; later, reply rate on consented threads.

**D4 — "From message to paperwork without retyping."**
- ES: *Del mensaje al papeleo sin volver a escribir.*
- Inquiry → deal → 130-U prefilled → check result. Show REVIEW REQUIRED, not a green checkmark.
- Measure: Apohenia packet trials.

**D5 — "Prove you're fast at registration."**
- ES: *Demuestra que registras rápido.*
- Registration milestones with dates; the buyer sees the same screen. Good dealers should be able to prove it.
- Guardrail: metrics with sources only; no ranking claims until data exists.

**B1 — "What can I actually get?"** (buyer)
- ES: *¿Qué me alcanza de verdad?*
- 6-second Reel: four taps, ring fills, green list appears. Text on screen: "No credit pull."
- Guardrail: no "qualify", "approve", "rate", "lender". Estimate language only. Keeps the ad out of the credit category [I: confirm with Meta review].
- Measure: completed flows, inquiries sent, cost per inquiry.

**B2 — "Know before you go."**
- ES: *Sabe antes de ir.*
- Static + Reel: the payment range, the down payment, the term, on one card. "Every fee on one screen before you sign" (registry entry required).
- Measure: completed flows.

**B3 — "The deal doesn't disappear after you buy."**
- ES: *La compra no desaparece después de comprar.*
- My Garage: documents, registration status, who sold it. Runs only after S004/S005 ship.

**B4 — "Fair prices, real people."** (Hispanic households, culturally authentic cast, Spanish-first with English version)
- ES: *Precios justos, gente de verdad.*
- Verified reviews from real transactions; verified dealer identity (GDN). Guardrail: no comparative claims against named competitors; bare "Verified" banned, say "GDN-verified dealer".

## 5. Channels beyond Meta
- **TIADA** (Texas Independent Automobile Dealers Association): events, newsletter, member list; the highest-trust B2B channel.
- **Auctions** (Manheim, ADESA, local): dealers are physically there weekly; a founding-cohort flyer with a QR to the application page.
- **YouTube pre-roll** on car-shopping content, Spanish and English (86% Hispanic reach).
- **TikTok dealer-life content**: authentic lot video outperforms polish; one independent reported ~1,000 leads/month from consistent posting [V]. Obavia's founding dealers can be the cast.
- **SEO "realistic fit" pages**: "$400 a month with fair credit in Houston" pages generated from the engine; evergreen, zero CAC.
- **Referral loop**: dealer refers dealer (founding badge), buyer refers buyer (nothing paid; a free product needs no incentive beyond usefulness).

## 6. Launch sequence
**Phase 0 (now, 2–4 weeks): landing + founding application.** Reuse EXP-01: bilingual page, "You are the whole dealership", application → 20-minute interview → founding pilot. Buyer side: the live fit tool is the landing page; add a phone-number waitlist for "tell me when dealers near me are on". Collect: dealership, location, inventory size, current systems, primary pain, monthly transactions, language. Never credit or ID data. Target: 10 interviews, 5 dealers who will run one real transaction.
**Phase 1 (weeks 4–12): concierge.** 10–15 Houston dealers on the workspace with Triple J as tenant zero; inquiries hand-matched; weekly calls; Apohenia checks on real packets. Gate: ≥10 paying, <5%/mo churn.
**Phase 2 (months 3–6): automation + buyer acquisition.** AI follow-up under consent; B1/B2 Reels at $2–5k/week; SEO fit pages; dealer cohort to 50. Gate: ≥50 paying, positive contribution margin.
**Phase 3 (months 6–12): marketplace layer public; reviews; registration tracker; Dallas/San Antonio.**
**Phase 4 (12+): app wrapper when there is an active-deal install reason; BHPH views; private seller lane.**
Two-sided rule [V]: supply first; throttle buyer onboarding to inventory depth (about 1 buyer per 5 listings early) so the first buyers are not disappointed.

## 7. Pricing
Constraints: NL-4 (never per lead or per sale), anti-brokering (flat subscription), constitution #2 (buyers free forever), ADR-0003 proposed shape.
Comparables [V]: Frazer $129/mo core ($300–$600 with add-ons); DealerCenter $60–$300; DealerSocket IDMS $179; Podium $249–$599; Kenect ~$150–$300 [I]; CarGurus avg ≈ $2,600/mo; Autotrader $450–$5,000. Small lots cite ~$400/mo as the ceiling [I].
Discount data [V]: customers acquired at 30%+ discount churn 4.2x faster; lifetime deals fail on support and API cost escalation.

| Option | Shape | Verdict |
|---|---|---|
| A (recommended) | Buyer $0 · Dealer marketplace $0 · Workspace $199/location/mo · founding $99 × 24 months, first 25–50 Texas dealers · metered pass-through (SMS, identity, e-sign, history reports, ad spend) · Apohenia per packet | Fits every constraint; below the $400 ceiling; founding badge permanent, price not |
| B | $0/mo + revenue share per closed sale | **Rejected**: NL-4 and brokering exposure |
| C | Buyer premium $4.99/mo + dealer tiers | **Rejected**: constitution #2 |
Billable unit begins when the dealer activates the document/operating workflow, never on a sale. All numbers are hypotheses; test them in the founding interviews.

## 9. Risks specific to marketing
- Ad copy drifts into credit language and gets restricted or violates NL-3. Weekly copy audit against the claims registry.
- Spanish creative underperforms because it is translated, not native. Use native Houston speakers; test culturally relevant English too.
- Founding price anchors the market at $99. Cap the cohort and state the term on every page.
- Lifetime-deal expectation from early adopters. Never say "forever".
- Two-sided ratings gamed early. Verified transaction required; moderation budget from Phase 3.
- Conflict of interest: Triple J is disclosed as founding design partner and excluded from any ranking it could influence (ADR-0006 proposed).
