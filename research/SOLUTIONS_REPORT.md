# Obavia: The Solution to Everything
### The constructive mirror to the Pessimist's Case (September 18, 2026)

## TL;DR
- Every pessimist objection has a concrete answer that keeps Obavia legally clean: launch as a dealer-paid compliance/paperwork SaaS (the Apohenia wedge); never touch buyer funds; never submit to webDEALER for a fee; never pull credit; never tie fees to leads/sales; never let AI make binding factual commitments — deferring marketplace, prequal, and money movement until licensed and capitalized.
- The regulatory walls are navigable: TrueCar survived anti-brokering by moving to flat-fee subscriptions; Transp. Code §520.063 exempts GDN-holding dealers and their employees from title-service licensing; Stripe Connect lets the dealer be merchant of record; UETA (Bus. & Com. Code §322.007) validates e-signatures; TRAIGA offers a NIST AI RMF safe harbor with a 60-day cure.
- Unit economics work at $99–$199 because heavy costs are metered pass-through: Twilio SMS ~$0.013/msg all-in; Claude Haiku 4.5 $1/$5 per MTok; Stripe Identity $1.50/verification; BoldSign $0.75/envelope.

## Key findings
1. The graveyard died of capital dependence (inventory, credit lines, CAC), not the idea. Obavia holds no inventory, no paper, no credit line, and is cash-flow-anchored to a paid dealer product from month one.
2. The regulations are a moat competitors with per-lead/DMS-of-record models structurally cannot cross.
3. Apohenia is Obavia in miniature — the transaction/document state machine. Staged expansion of one data model, not scope creep.
4. Market: ~117k independent locations; ~52k genuinely active; 9.8M units in 2025. Large even conservatively.

## Why this survives where the graveyard didn't
No inventory, no credit line, no capital dependence; cash-flow-positive wedge from month one; a compliance moat incumbents' business models cannot cross; overlay that partners with the DMS; AI liability engineered out; Stripe as licensed transmitter with the dealer as MoR; and a founder roadmap sequenced to reality — Apohenia now, Workspace next, marketplace only when dealers already pay the bills.

## Caveats
Some pricing figures are third-party estimates. Money-transmission and title-service exemptions depend on exact configuration — Texas MSB/dealer counsel review before reliance.
