# Obavia product constitution (founder-approved constraints)
Change only via a recorded decision in docs/decisions/DECISIONS.md.

1. Name: Obavia.
2. Buyers are free throughout the core journey: search, save, message, participate in supported applications, sign, retrieve documents, track purchase/registration, review. No buyer platform fee, ever, at any screen.
3. Continuity: the person who inquired becomes the customer in the deal, signs the correct documents, receives a purchase record, and can review — without restarting in another system.
4. Dealers can participate (verified profile, listings, inquiries, sale records, reviews) without adopting a full operating system or migrating their DMS.
5. Paperwork, payment, delivery, and registration are separate states. "Sale completed" never hides unfinished obligations. No single `sold` boolean.
6. Explicit permissions and human authority. A dealership sees its authorized customer relationships, not every Obavia user. AI assists; humans commit.
7. English and Spanish are first-class paths, not translations bolted on.
8. UX is a priority: mobile-first web; no app install required to finish a transaction.
9. Verification distinguishes identity from authority. No single generic "Verified" badge.
10. Feedback: two-sided, one finalized record per author per verified transaction; append-only corrections with history; no suppression tools. Dealer→buyer feedback is operational only (showed up, responsive, documents on time, respectful), never payment or default data, visible to other dealers only in aggregate with the buyer's consent. No public buyer financial reputation (ADR-0011).
11. The never-list (see obavia-texas-guardrails) is a constitutional constraint until Obavia is licensed and capitalized to change it.
12. Apohenia (the pre-submission Texas deal-packet checker) is Obavia's Stage 0 — the document/deal state machine at the core of the transaction object. It is not a separate product to be abandoned.
