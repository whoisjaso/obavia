# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

OBAVIA serves private clients, assistants, visiting executives, founders, and members who need discreet access to executive vehicles without a public rental-counter experience. A secondary user is the internal operations team managing vehicles, bookings, customers, payments, and membership status.

## Product Purpose

OBAVIA is a private luxury vehicle rental house. The public site should create trust and desire through restraint, while the booking, member, and admin surfaces should make the service feel precise, calm, and easy to operate.

Success means a visitor understands the offer quickly, can browse the curated fleet, request access or book a vehicle, and can see how membership and operations work without the interface feeling like a mainstream rental site.

## Brand Personality

Discreet, curated, dependable.

The emotional goal is quiet confidence: private members club, modern heritage house, private bank, and luxury watch discipline.

## Anti-references

Avoid dealership pages, airport rental counters, discount travel apps, loud ecommerce grids, racing imagery, supercar culture, neon nightlife, chrome effects, shields, crowns, stars, wings, speed lines, and checkered flags.

Avoid generic startup dashboard styling, blue SaaS metrics, purple glow gradients, oversized rounded cards, noisy filters, and decorative filler copy.

## Design Principles

1. Privacy is the luxury signal: reduce noise, badges, and unnecessary explanations.
2. The vehicle is curated, not sold: use editorial presentation over price-heavy merchandising.
3. Every surface should feel handled: booking, member, and admin workflows need clear hierarchy and restrained controls.
4. The monogram carries the brand: use the O mark as a quiet system element, not as ornament.
5. The palette stays disciplined: deep ink black, warm ivory, and muted antique gold only.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA contrast for all text and controls. Support keyboard focus states, reduced motion, responsive layouts from mobile to desktop, readable form labels, and non-color-only selected states.
