This Membership Agreement (this “Agreement”) is entered into as of the Effective Date between {{ entityName }}, a Texas limited liability company at {{ companyAddress }} (the “Company”), and the individual named above (the “Member”). 2.  Fee & billing

2.1  Monthly fee. The Member’s tier and its monthly fee are shown above and bill on a recurring monthly cycle until cancelled. The first month is charged when the membership begins.

2.2  Card on file — the only method. Membership is billed to a valid, non-prepaid debit or credit card on file. Debit is fully accepted; only prepaid and gift cards are excluded. The Member authorizes the Company to charge that card automatically each cycle. No cash, checks, peer-to-peer transfers, or prepaid cards are accepted.

2.3  Surcharge. Any card surcharge applies to credit cards only and never to debit, and will not exceed the Company’s actual cost of acceptance.

3.  The member benefit

3.1  Rate discount. While the membership is active, the Member’s tier discount applies automatically to the weekly rate of the vehicle the Member rents. The discount is a percentage off that vehicle’s rate and is reflected on each weekly charge.

3.2  No stacking with the Hardship Bridge. The member discount does not stack with the Hardship Bridge. In any week both could apply, the Member receives whichever is more favorable — never both.

3.3  Exotic & Noir access. Discounts and priority access to exotic and Noir-class vehicles are reserved for the Black Key tier. Other tiers may rent those classes at standard rates where available.

What this means
Your membership saves you money every week you drive, and you always get the better of your member rate or the Hardship Bridge. The savings scale with the car — the nicer the vehicle, the more your percentage is worth.

4.  Founding rate

Early Keyholder members may lock the founding rate of {{ foundingFee }}/month for life, held for as long as the membership stays active. The regular Keyholder price is a bona fide {{ foundingRegular }}/month — the genuine price later members pay. There is no fake “was” price and no countdown. The founding rate is available once per verified identity; if the membership lapses or is cancelled, the founding rate is not guaranteed on re-enrollment.

5.  Member perks

Active members receive the benefits published for their tier, which may include income protection during a covered vehicle outage, priority access and complimentary upgrades, expedited re-rental, unit swaps, mileage and expense reports, and loyalty milestones. The Company lists a benefit only once it is genuinely available and never advertises a benefit it cannot deliver. Benefits may be added over time and apply automatically to active members; they are not a guarantee of any specific outcome.

5.1  Credit reporting. Where offered, on-time payments may be reported to consumer reporting agencies, so renting in good standing can help the Member build credit. Reporting is informational and not a promise of any particular credit result.

6.  Cancellation — no traps

6.1  Cancel anytime. The Member may cancel at any time, with no penalty, no retention maze, and no phone call required. Membership remains active through the end of the current paid month and does not renew after that.

6.2  Reverting to standard. When membership ends or lapses, the Member’s rate simply reverts to the standard published rate immediately. Any vehicle rental already in place continues on its own terms; only the membership discount stops.

7.  One per member

Membership is personal to the Member and non-transferable. One membership is permitted per verified identity, backed by a valid non-prepaid card. The founding rate is available once per identity. Sharing, reselling, or transferring a membership or its benefits is not permitted and may end the membership.

8.  General

This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of Texas, with venue in Harris County, Texas; the English-language version controls. The Company may update tier pricing or benefits prospectively on notice; a Member’s locked founding rate is honored for as long as the membership stays active. This Agreement, together with any rental agreement the Member signs, is the entire understanding on its subject.
