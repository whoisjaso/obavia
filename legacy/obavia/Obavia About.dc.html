About OBAVIA

A car rental house that treats you like a guest, not a risk.

OBAVIA rents premium and everyday vehicles by the week in Houston — to the drivers most companies screen out, and for the occasions that deserve something finer. The respect is the luxury.

Most rental companies are built to say no.

A thin credit file, a gig income, a card that isn't a platinum — and the door closes. We built OBAVIA for the people on the other side of that door: rideshare and delivery drivers whose week is the car, families replacing one that's in the shop, and anyone who simply wants a beautiful vehicle for a weekend that matters.

Our standards are real and they're published — not a mood the front desk is in. Meet them and you're a member, welcomed the same whether you're driving a Corolla on Monday or a Mercedes on Friday.
