# Obavia — product
**Promise.** Obavia makes good dealers easier to trust and serious buyers easier to serve. Buyer: *Buying a car shouldn't leave you guessing.* Dealer: *You already have enough to do. Obavia keeps the deal moving.*

**What it is.** The user-facing orchestration and truth layer across a vehicle transaction: discovery → realistic fit → verified dealer contact → conversation → appointment → terms → application → deal → paperwork → signatures → delivery → registration → ownership record → servicing → review. Most automotive products own a stage; Obavia owns continuity.

**Users.** (1) Texas independent dealer owner-operators (10–80 units/mo, often one person wearing seven hats; BHPH common; EN/ES). (2) Buyers in one launch metro (Houston), including subprime, Spanish-speaking, first-time, co-signers. (3) Later: private sellers, lenders as participants (never as Obavia's own credit).

**Participation lanes.** Buyer/Owner free · Dealer Marketplace free · Dealer Workspace paid · Private Seller (later, separate lane).

**Stage 0 = Apohenia.** The pre-submission Texas deal-packet checker (three states: NO KNOWN BLOCKER DETECTED / REVIEW REQUIRED / BLOCKING ISSUE DETECTED; approval-implying language banned) is the first paid wedge and the seed of the deal/document state machine. It is in a $500 founding pilot. Obavia grows from it; it does not replace it.

**Non-goals (v1).** Holding money · submitting to webDEALER for a fee · pulling credit · per-lead/per-sale fees · autonomous AI commitments · national availability · DMS replacement · public buyer financial scores · private-party title workflow · 50-state paperwork.

**Positioning rules.** Never "dealers are shady." Never "nobody is buying cars." The dry-lot theme is an ad hypothesis ("Does your lot feel slower than it used to?"), not a factual claim.

**Naming note.** The OBAVIA rental-membership site that previously lived in this repo is a different business line (Triple J rental fleet). Whether it keeps the Obavia name is an open founder decision (ADR-0007).
