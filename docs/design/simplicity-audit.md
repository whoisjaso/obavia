How to read the tables. **Act**: the words a person must read to do the one thing (count, then the words). **Ten-year-old**: every word over two syllables on the glass, and the count of sentences; a car's name and the law's own words are exempt (rule 11). **Before**: the things a stranger must look at before the verb (rule 12: more than two is a split). **Split**: when the screen holds two things, the pages it becomes, in order, each with its one thing. **Cut**: the words that leave, then the words that stay (four or fewer per line, three or fewer to act). **Next piece**: what touches what so the next step is the person's idea, with no arrow and no hint (rule 13). **Reward**: what the body gets inside 100 ms of the good tap as built, with the file and line, and what the big moments should get (rule 14). **Copart**: what Copart makes the person find or read that Obavia deletes (`docs/plan/copart-decoded.md`).
