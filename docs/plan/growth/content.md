# Content list: four quadrants, dealers and buyers

Written October 12, 2026 by marketing, with creative-edge and onboarding. Owned by creative-edge (status, play, engagement) and marketing (topics, placement). This is the file the growth plan points to for CP2, CP6, CP7, CP10, AD5, AD6 and AD9.

**Read this first.** The brief asked for twelve dealer breakouts. The plan says something different, so this file follows the plan. Plan §1.3, CP6: "Six breakouts for buyers (the six questions the desk is tired of answering, onboarding mandate) and six for dealers. … Twelve scripted by Fri Nov 6; cut by Fri Nov 20, 2026." The week 6 gate's "twelve breakouts cut" means six dealer breakouts and six buyer breakouts. They are marked **G6** below. Every other piece in this file is extra. Extra pieces may be scripted, but none of them is filmed before the twelve G6 pieces are cut. Twelve dealer breakouts would need a dated amendment to plan §5 first.

Standing rules for every piece (plan, "Standing rules" and §4):
- No testimonials, star ratings, review links or named buyers presented as typical. The worst case is shown instead (CP10).
- No income claim, no "you will make" and no hammer forecast. A past result is shown only as a dated number from the published pilot results, with the line "Ten cars are not a forecast for yours."
- Each title is the question the piece answers. Thumbnails state the same question. No clickbait.
- Every number on screen carries a source line from the register in §4. A claim marked UNVERIFIED is cut from the piece until researcher-architect lands a source in `docs/plan/research/<topic>.md`. Saying "UNVERIFIED" on camera is not an option.
- No vendor is named on screen unless counsel clears the comparison (`dealer-pain-ads.md`, rules).
- Buyer copy never says "dealer price", "wholesale price" or "below retail". 43 TAC §215.85(b)(6) as quoted in `obavia-auction-decisions.md` §0 restricts advertising a special discounted price. Counsel question 7 is open.
- Reduced motion is respected in every cut. Captions are burned in, because no piece needs sound to be understood.
