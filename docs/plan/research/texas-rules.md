# Texas and federal rules the lane and the Desk rely on (register S01, S07, S12, S17, S18, S29)

Researcher-architect, checked 2026-10-12. The register in `growth/content.md` §4 names separate files (`gdn.md`, `tax.md`, `registration.md`, `form-8300.md`, `counsel.md`). They are answered here in one file, and `README.md` maps each row to its section.

## Question

What exactly do 43 TAC §215.85, the dealer title-filing duty, the FTC Used Car Rule, the Texas motor vehicle sales tax, the title and registration fees, the insurance-at-registration rule and the Form 8300 trigger say today? Do the Desk's seeded values match?

## Answer

§215.85 reads as `obavia-auction-decisions.md` §0 quotes it. The full text also adds that dealing "directly or indirectly" includes consignment, and it was last amended effective 6/1/2024. §215.144, the neighbouring rule, contains a line the plan has not met: **"A GDN holder may not sell another GDN holder's vehicle at a public motor vehicle auction."** That line reaches Phase 3 directly. The dealer's title duty is in Tex. Transp. Code §501.0234(a). The statute itself says only "in the time and manner provided by law" and "a reasonable time" (its old fixed-day subsection was repealed in 2022). **The 30 days, and 45 days for a dealer-financed sale, come from 43 TAC §215.144(f)(3).** So the house documents should cite the rule, not the statute, for the day count, and the `dealer-pain-ads.md` attribution of "45 days" to 501.0234 is wrong. The sales tax is 6¼% of total consideration under Tax Code §152.021(b). That matches `0.0625`. The title fee is **$33 only in a nonattainment or affected county and $28 elsewhere** (Transp. Code §501.138). Harris County is in the Houston-Galveston-Brazoria nonattainment area, so $33 is right for the Houston pilot and wrong for most of the state. **$75 registration is not a TxDMV figure.** TxDMV lists a $50.75 base fee for a passenger car (shown as a $51.75 registration fee, with $1 for TexasSure). With the processing fee ($4.75), the inspection replacement fee ($7.50), the emissions fee where it applies ($2.75) and a county local fee of $0–$31.50, the total is **$64.00 to $98.25**. Evidence of financial responsibility is required before a county will register the car (Transp. Code §502.046(a)). Under the FTC Used Car Rule, a dealer must display a Buyers Guide on the vehicle before offering it to a consumer (16 CFR §455.2(a)). The Guide's contents override the sales contract (§455.3(b)), and a sale conducted in Spanish needs the Spanish Guide (§455.5). Form 8300 is due within 15 days for cash over $10,000. In a car sale, "cash" includes cashier's checks of $10,000 or less.

## The decisions it forces

1. **§215.144(i)(3) reaches the Phase 3 plan.** If Obavia holds a GDN and runs the lane as a public motor vehicle auction, Obavia cannot sell another GDN holder's car on it. "Other dealers join under it" (decisions §0, step 2) is blocked until counsel answers whether the lane is a "public motor vehicle auction" and whether a title-assigned consignment (§215.144(h)) changes the answer. The pilot (Triple J selling its own cars) is not affected. This is written into `obavia-auction-decisions.md` §8.
2. The Desk's `TEXAS = { taxRate: 0.0625, title: 33, registration: 75 }` (`desk/src/lib/config.ts`, `auction/src/engine/money.ts`):
   - taxRate 0.0625: **matches** §152.021(b).
   - title 33: **matches only nonattainment and affected counties**, which includes Harris. The default should come from the buyer's county: $33 or $28.
   - registration 75: **not a TxDMV figure.** The real amount is $64.00–$98.25 by county and emissions status. Until per-county values exist, the out-the-door total must say "estimate" next to registration. The high end ($98.25) is the conservative default.
3. Every house document that gives the 30-day title duty cites **43 TAC §215.144(f)(3)** under Transp. Code §501.0234(f), with 45 days only for dealer-financed sales.
4. The Form 8300 trigger in the Desk counts cashier's checks and money orders of $10,000 or less as cash in a car sale (decisions §2 row 6 says "over $10,000 cash" only).
