# Buyer urgency: one dated, third-party fact (register S27)

Researcher-architect, checked 2026-10-12. This file answers `growth/content.md` §4 row S27 and chapter 2 of the buyer urgency video (§3.2).

## Question

Is there one third-party fact about Texas or US used-car prices or wholesale supply that carries a date and a publisher, that a buyer can check, and that is not a discount or a forecast?

## Answer

Yes, but it is national. No authoritative Texas figure was found. Use this one, with "US" stated: **"US dealers' average used-vehicle listing price was $27,239 in August 2026, 7% above a year ago." Cox Automotive, from vAuto Live Market View data, published Sep. 11, 2026.** The same release gives a second fact that fits Obavia's buyer more closely: used vehicles under $15,000 were **15.1% of US used-vehicle inventory in August 2026, down from 20.6% a year earlier**, with **29 days' supply**. Either fact can be checked on the publisher's page. Use one of them on screen, not both. The listing-price fact matches the frame the video already uses ("That's what lots are asking"). On the wholesale side, the Manheim Used Vehicle Value Index for September 2026 was **205.9, down 0.6% year over year and down 1.1% from August** (Cox Automotive, Oct. 7, 2026). That index measures what dealers pay at auction, not what the viewer pays, so it is the wrong fact for a buyer video. It is recorded here for the dealer side and for `wholesale-band.md`.

## The decision it forces

1. Chapter 2 of the buyer video uses: headline "Used-Vehicle Prices Reach Highest Level Since 2022 as Inventory Tightens in August", publisher "Cox Automotive", date "Sep. 11, 2026", figure "$27,239 average US used listing price, August 2026". The word **US** is on screen. Then the frame: "That's what lots are asking. Here, the price is whatever the bidders say, and you can see it live."
2. Do not write "prices are rising" or "prices will rise". The September wholesale index fell, so a trend claim would be one-sided. The figure is shown as a dated level, never as a direction or a forecast.
3. Do not put the $27,239 next to any lot's bid. That would make it a price comparison, which counsel question 7 (Retail bar as a price representation) and 43 TAC §215.85(b)(6) (no special discounted price) both reach.
4. Before filming, re-check whether a newer month has been published. If it has, use the newest month with its own date. Never put two months side by side.
5. Register S27 moves from UNVERIFIED to **sourced (US, August 2026)**.
