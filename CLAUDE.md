# OBAVIA — Project Memory

OBAVIA is a private luxury vehicle rental house in Houston serving **premium gig drivers + luxury/exotic occasions**. Standard weekly access is framed as *earned membership*. See `DESIGN.md` / `PRODUCT.md` for palette, type, and components.

## Locked visual system
- **Display / wordmark:** Bodoni Moda (Didone). (Older files used Cormorant — Bodoni is the current direction; it satisfies the doctrine's "refined serif display.")
- **UI / nav / labels:** Montserrat (600 nav, 500 subtitle). **Body / small copy:** Helvetica Neue / Inter.
- **Color:** ink `#121212`, ivory `#F5F0E6`, gold `#b89b5e`. Gold is **jewelry, not paint** — linework, selected states, small metadata only.
- **Motion:** slow, deliberate, expensive. Fades + gentle parallax/drift at the pace of a confident walk. **Nothing bounces, nothing flashes.** Respect `prefers-reduced-motion`.
- **Menu ambience:** YouTube track `NW8Cxc3uh5E` plays (clean, hidden, looped) on menu-open, stops on close, gated by the AMBIENCE toggle.

## Psychological Operating Doctrine (load-bearing — NEVER stated on the site)
Four ancestral entry points + two amplifiers. Design serves these silently.

**1. NOVELTY — capture focus by breaking the pattern**
- Landing is an editorial, not a car grid: one full-bleed cinematic vehicle/scene at Houston dusk, vast negative space, one serif line, one action. Inventory lives one level deeper.
- One vehicle at a time: full-screen, identically shot (same angles/light/backdrop), presented like a watch listing. A $5K Corolla shot like a $90K S-Class IS the pattern interrupt.
- Names, not stock numbers: e.g. "Corolla — Unit 04 · Slate," same typographic dignity across tiers.
- No marketplace tropes anywhere: no starbursts, "HOT DEAL," red strikethroughs, urgency banners. Their absence is the signal.

**2. AUTHORITY — restraint, order, visible standards**
- Restraint is the master aesthetic. Quiet luxury, enormous margins. If a screen feels empty, it's correct.
- The **Standards page** is the authority centerpiece: the published screening matrix (identity, driving record, insurance) per tier, shown openly and beautifully like a club's published dress code. "Here is the bar," no apology.
- Precision on every number: exact rates/fees/dates in one confident table.
- Never raise the voice. Consequence/policy copy = calm, fair inevitability (a five-star hotel's utility bill). The restoration path is always stated beside any consequence.

**3. TRIBE — earned belonging + a visible ladder**
- Customers are **members**, earned by passing the published standards.
- Tiers as a visible ladder with stated ascent criteria: **Standard / Reserve (SUV & XL) / Obavia Noir (luxury)**.
- Member Dashboard = standing as identity: tier, member-since, renter-score band (refined progress element), and prominently *what the next band unlocks* (newer units, tier access, float-vehicle priority, ownership-path eligibility). Ladder-up gets ≥ the weight of any balance owed.
- Flagship benefit, plainly stated: **"Your on-time payments are reported. Renting with Obavia builds your credit."**
- Social proof only honest + aggregate; never fabricated, never individual without consent.

**4. EMOTION — decision is emotional; arc = relief → dignity → aspiration**
- **Relief** first in booking: "No deposit on Standard. Your insurance, your terms, published upfront. Approved members drive within 48 hours."
- **Dignity** in EVERY state incl. delinquency: dunning screens use the same type/tone/palette as marketing. No red alarm screens. Show the exact cure path + exact dates in the same calm voice that sold the car.
- **Aspiration** always visible: Noir vehicles appear as horizon, not paywalled. The arc: ~$300/wk Corolla → luxury weekend → outright ownership (BHPH: members in good standing may purchase from the fleet on house terms).
- Photography: Houston-real, golden hour, believable member contexts. Aspirational but attainable — never lifestyle stock.

**5. EXPECTANCY (amplifier) — make the future specific + certain**
- Future-paced onboarding copy: "By Friday you're driving. By month three your payment history is on your credit file. By month twelve Reserve reviews automatically."
- Process certainty everywhere: every flow shows where the member stands and exactly what happens next and when (review times, handoff windows, consequence countdowns with exact dates). Ambiguity is what cheap feels like.

**6. RECIPROCITY (amplifier) — give first**
- Free, genuinely useful resources before any commitment: a one-page "renting smart in Houston" guide (tolls, insurance basics, delivery-driver tax notes); a transparent cost calculator showing total weekly cost incl. every fee before signup.

## Membership program (locked — `obavia-membership.js` is the single source of truth)
- **Tiers (distinct from vehicle classes Standard/Reserve/Noir):** **Open** (free, 0%, standard rates) · **Keyholder** ($29.99/mo, 5% off everyday fleet) · **Black Key** ($199.99/mo, 20% off every class + Noir/exotic access). Key metaphor = car-brand prestige ladder; Black Key = black-card energy.
- **Founding Member** is the founding-RATE label for early Keyholders ($29.99 locked for life), NOT a tier. Anchor: regular Keyholder price is a bona fide **$79.99** (the real future price) — honest reference pricing, no fake strikethrough, no countdown timers (FTC/DTPA daylight test).
- **Discount** = % off the per-vehicle weekly rate, automatic while active; **does NOT stack with Hardship Bridge** — apply whichever is better that week (`effectiveWeekly()`). Exotic/Noir discount + access gated to Black Key.
- **Perks, two categories.** A (OBAVIA-controlled, always live): member rate, **Zero-Downtime Guarantee** = income protection = headline, priority access + free upgrades, skip-the-line/skip re-verify, swap, mileage/expense reports, skip-a-week pause, loyalty milestones. B (partner/back-end-pending — built in architecture but **feature-flagged OFF in the customer view** until real; visible in admin): fuel discount, rideshare insurance discount, gig-essential discounts, referral credits. `obavia_perk_flags` in localStorage; never advertise an undeliverable benefit.
- **Anti-abuse:** one membership per verified identity, non-transferable, no prepaid cards, founding price once per identity, cancel/lapse → standard rate immediately, easy revert (no traps).
- **Surfaces:** standalone **OBAVIA Membership.dc.html** is the sales home (linked from public Home + surfaced at point of rental — highest-conversion moment). Member status & management live in the Customer Portal. Admin shows tier badge everywhere a renter appears + member-since/next-billing/discount% + Cat-B flag-state panel, driven by the same engine. Demo persona John Lawrence Doe = founding Keyholder.

## Confirmed contract terms (locked — use these exact values in BOTH rental agreements)
- **Grace period:** three (3) calendar days before any late fee attaches.
- **Late fee:** **$75.00** flat per occurrence — the same amount wherever a late fee appears.
- **Mileage:** unlimited, unless a specific mileage limit is stated in writing.
- **Toll/citation admin fee:** **$30.00** flat per item processed (on top of the pass-through cost).
- **Out-of-state use:** prohibited — Vehicle must remain within Texas for the whole Rental Period.
- Files: `Obavia Weekly Rental Agreement.dc.html` and `Obavia Rental Agreement (Portal Edition).dc.html`.
- **Contract coherence (done):** Portal-Edition reads entity/address/rate/late-fee/grace/surcharge/bridge from Settings (`obavia_settings_v2`) via a `SET` merge in `renderVals` → holes `entityName`/`entityLegal`/`companyAddress`/`weeklyRate*`/`bridgePctStr`/`bridgeFloorStr`. Weekly draft corrected statically ($250→$350, placeholder entity → OBAVIA). **`Obavia Membership Agreement.dc.html`** = short standalone membership contract (Settings-bound entity/address + membership-engine-bound tier table/founding rate; card-only, no-stacking, no-traps cancel, one-per-identity; same Didone/ivory print system + height-auto print fix).
- **Contract client-language courtesy (done):** Portal-Edition reads `?lang=es|vi` (or `obavia_lang`) and renders a translated **Key Terms** summary panel (rate/payment-card-only/grace+late-fee/mileage/TX-only/insurance/devices/cancellation, values from Settings) above the agreement, with an "English version controls" notice. Self-contained `AGR` dict in the contract logic (en/es/vi) — NOT full operative-clause translation (English controlling per doctrine + i18n DRAFT flag). `showCourtesy` true only for es/vi.
- **Print fix (load-bearing):** the DC runtime pins `html,body,#dc-root` to `height:100%` on the standalone/print boot, which clips Chrome to ONE page. Each agreement's `@media print` must force `html, body, #dc-root, #dc-root > .sc-host, main, .doc { height:auto !important; min-height:0 !important; overflow:visible !important }`. Keep this on any new printable doc.
- **THE deployed contract is the one EMBEDDED IN THE ADMIN** (`OBAVIA Admin.dc.html` → rental → "View & print signed agreement"), which `dc-import`s the Portal-Edition agreement inside a `position:fixed; overflow:auto` overlay. The standalone agreement file is just a scratch surface for tweaking. **Printing must work FROM the admin.** Admin `@media print` (in its `<helmet>`) isolates the contract: `[data-app-root]:has([data-pdf-overlay]) > *:not([data-pdf-overlay]){display:none}` (NOT visibility:hidden — that leaves blank pages) + un-fix `[data-pdf-overlay]` to `position:static;overflow:visible;height:auto` + un-cap `[data-pdf-sheet]`. The agreement's own injected print CSS (Letter `@page`+margins, running `.run-head`/`.run-foot` letterhead every page, `break-inside:avoid` on p/li/clauses, `h1–h4 break-after:avoid`, `.sig-grid` intact, `.page-break` before signatures, `.center-page` reserved for server-side orphan centering) then paginates it. Markers `data-app-root`, `data-pdf-overlay`, `data-pdf-sheet`, `data-pdf-actions` are load-bearing — keep them. Print button = plain `window.print()` (never bounce to a new tab).

## Admin operations console (`OBAVIA Admin.dc.html`) — locked mechanics
- **Rate lives on the vehicle, not globally.** `$350/wk` is the **Standard-class default** only; Reserve ~$480, Noir far higher (seed S 580 @ $1,950). `CLASS_RATE` seeds the Add-Vehicle form; the per-vehicle rate is authoritative and auto-flows into any rental written on that unit. Settings "Standard-class default rate" only prefills new Standard units.
- **Auto unit numbering:** units are assigned sequentially (`Unit 01`, `02`, …) from a monotonic `obavia_unit_seq` counter — never typed by anyone, **permanent, never reused** (retiring a unit does not free its number). Persisted fleet in `obavia_fleet`; seed reseeds on first load. Display as "Unit 0N · Model"; plate/VIN is the true identity.
- **Hardship Bridge is a per-vehicle/per-tier flag** (`bridgeEligible`), offered on economy/Standard only — never auto-applied to Reserve/Noir. Gated in the New-Rental terms step and on the vehicle record.
- **Rental row → Rental Detail** holds the full record: snapshot (rep, billing, started, duration, next charge), People & vehicle deep-links, Insurance & identity, Agreed terms (incl. out-of-state Prohibited · TX only), Ledger (empty-until-real), Timeline, signature record + signed-contract view/print, and status-appropriate actions (resend/copy link · counter-sign · renew/end/message).
- **Documents** is the searchable signed-contract archive (by client / unit / date). Deep-linking is one tap each way: rental ↔ client ↔ vehicle ↔ contract. Role-scoped throughout; single labeled demo persona (John Lawrence Doe) carries the clickthrough.
- **Inventory (renamed from Fleet) → Vehicle Detail (one click in).** Table stays lean (Unit · Vehicle · Color · Plate · Tier · Rate · Status); depth lives in the record: Identity (VIN/plate/YMM/trim), VIN-decoded Specs (engine+cyl/fuel/drivetrain/transmission/body), Mileage (odometer, owner+maint editable), Rate+Bridge, derived Status, Listing photos + Condition photos (both `<image-slot>` — listing feeds public site, condition captured at handoff), Maintenance & service (last/next + mechanic notes, owner+maint editable), History (this unit's rentals, deep-linked), Owner-only (acquisition cost, in-house note terms, title/reg/inspection). Seed has `SEED_VERSION` — bump it to force a reseed when vehicle schema changes (`obavia_fleet_seed` marker in localStorage).
- **VIN decode = NHTSA vPIC** (`DecodeVinValues` endpoint, real client-side fetch, CORS-open, 8s AbortController timeout). Confirmed reachable from the live preview. Add-Vehicle is VIN-first (decode auto-fills specs) with a graceful manual-entry fallback; record has a "Re-decode". Make acronyms ≤3 chars stay uppercase (BMW/GMC/RAM). Add-Vehicle modal is a **centered dialog** (backdrop flex-center, card max-h 90vh), not a side panel. Role-scoped: Maintenance sees specs/service; Owner sees cost; Reps see rate/availability. Inventory table is sorted alphabetically by make (then model) on every render, so Add Vehicle auto-places a new unit in order; unit numbers stay permanent/sequential underneath.
- **Nav order:** primary admin nav is alphabetical by visible label with **Overview pinned first** (dashboard home), applied across roles via `navItems()` sort.
- **Payments console (`secPayments`, Owner + Finance; finance nav routes "payments" not "ledger" — old Ledger section removed).** Rate truth lives in **Settings → Rate configuration** (class defaults Standard/Reserve/Noir, Hardship Bridge $280/$100-floor, surcharge, membership config) — Payments *computes from* the per-vehicle rate, never sets it. Payments holds: At-a-glance (Collected $0 until real · Upcoming scheduled net of membership % · Overdue from the engine · Membership MRR) · event-sourced **ledger** (empty-until-real, "no imagined entries") · Upcoming scheduled (deep-linked, member-discount applied — John's S350 nets $332.50 at 5%) · Overdue & failed dunning · Membership MRR rows · one slim status line (automated · surcharge credit-only · refunds). Empty-until-real, demo persona John Lawrence Doe carries the seams. Backend (Stripe, ledger, scheduler, dunning) wired later by Claude Code.
- **CARD-ONLY across the system (locked, final).** Every renter provides a **valid, non-prepaid debit OR credit card on file** — debit fully accepted (gig-base accessibility), credit accepted, **only prepaid excluded** (burner/fraud vector). Prepaid detection is **BIN-based** (`isPrepaidCard()` in the portal — first-6 BIN vs known prepaid ranges; strong filter, not perfect; Claude Code wires the live BIN service + Stripe vaulting/auto-charge/subscriptions). Enforcement: **Payments** = purely automated card auto-charge, NO manual/cash rail, ledger "fully automated · nothing to reconcile" (the manual-payment modal + Cash/Zelle/Cash App were removed). **Portal onboarding step 5** vaults a non-prepaid card, rejects prepaid with a dignified notice, blocks advance/execute (`gateOk(5)`/`stepStatus(5)`/`canExecute` all require `!isPrepaidCard`). **New Rental flow**: intake qualifier "Valid debit or credit card on file (no prepaid)", terms row "Card on file · auto-charge (no prepaid)", and a terms-step gate note "no card, no execution". **Both contracts**: §4.3 "Sole accepted method — card on file" (non-prepaid debit/credit; no cash/checks/P2P/prepaid), §5.1 card-on-file auth, §5.2 surcharge credit-only (cash/Zelle/Cash App references removed). **Membership** = card-only subscription ("Billed to: your debit or credit card — no prepaid"). **Surcharge** credit-only, never debit (federal rule) everywhere. Anti-abuse: no prepaid, one card-backed verified identity, non-transferable.
- **Membership tracking is real + persisted** (`obavia_members` registry keyed by client name, seeded John = active founding Keyholder). `memberFor()` reads it; status `active` → tier discount + MRR apply, anything else (lapsed/cancelled/none) → Open / standard rate immediately (no-traps doctrine). `isActiveMember(name)` gates discounts. Discount/MRR/scheduled-charge math all derive from actual active status.
- **Overdue engine + dedicated Overdue page** (`secOverdue`, Owner + Finance, in nav). `overdueAccounts()` is member-aware: an account is overdue only when a scheduled weekly charge passes its due date + **3-day grace** without clearing; amount due = per-vehicle rate net of active-member discount + **$75** late fee, with exact cure date (dignity doctrine — calm voice, cure path always shown). Empty-until-real by default (no payment back-end ⇒ nothing genuinely overdue, "no imagined balances"). A persisted `obavia_demo_overdue` flag (toggle on the Overdue page header, OFF by default) injects the labeled demo persona (John, 8 days since due ⇒ 5 past grace, $332.50 + $75 = $407.50) so the flow is visible end to end. When overdue accounts exist they **prepend to Overview → Needs You** (salmon dot, "Review" → Overdue page) and drive the Payments Overdue tile. Late fee $75 / grace 3 days match the locked contract terms.

## Settings = single source of truth (`secSettings`, Owner-only, `OBAVIA Admin.dc.html`)
- **Principle:** every business rule the system reads lives here; nothing hardcoded/duplicated downstream (contracts, rentals, payments, onboarding, enforcement all read Settings). Kills the "two values fighting" fault class (rate-card vs per-vehicle, inconsistent late fee). `SETTINGS_DEFAULTS` object + `settings()` accessor (merges defaults with persisted `obavia_settings_v2`); `setSetting(group,key,value,label)` persists + appends to the `obavia_settings_log` change log (who/when/what, capped 50) + keeps `CLASS_RATE.Standard` in sync. Owner-only — non-owners get a gate message and Settings isn't in their nav.
- **7 grouped cards** (rendered as a clean single-column **accordion** — collapsed by default, tap a group to expand its fields; max-width 600px centered, one field per row, no nested input columns, mobile-clean / no horizontal scroll): (1) **Business & Legal** — entity (OBAVIA LLC), DBA, address, EIN, dealer license, contact, venue (Harris County TX), controlling language (English) → flows into every contract. (2) **Rates & Pricing** — Standard editable; Reserve/Noir defaults-only ("per-unit rate in Inventory is authoritative"); Hardship Bridge %/floor, surcharge % (credit-only), founding regular price + active toggle. (3) **Membership** — tiers/fees/% from the engine + Cat-B partner-perk feature flags. (4) **Payments & Billing** — card policy (no prepaid), single late-fee ($75), grace (3d), deposit, billing day. (5) **Onboarding & Identity** — IDV provider, 3-tier name-match thresholds, min age (21), ID types, data-retention, privacy contact. (6) **Enforcement & Timing** — dunning cadence, starter-interrupt notice, soft-hold, draft expiry, GPS + starter-interrupt toggles. (7) **Languages** — supported/default/controlling. Plus a **change-history** accordion row. Persistence + full log wired by Claude Code.

## Public Vehicles + application (`Obavia Vehicles.dc.html`) — Stage A of A→B→C→D
- **One continuous mobile-first scroll** (Bodoni/ink/gold, matches Home): cinematic **watch-listing** (one vehicle at a time, full-bleed gradient per tier, prev/next + dots) **+ a fast-path** (class filter chips Standard/Reserve/Noir + "Available only" toggle + "Browse all" quick-list). Cars read from **Inventory** (`obavia_fleet` localStorage; SEED fallback) — per-vehicle rate, class, derived availability (rented via active `obavia_agreements`, in-shop via `obavia_fleet_status`). Photos are gradient placeholders for now (real listing photos wire later).
- **Application unfolds below** — guided, **tap-to-advance (no scroll-jacking)**, one question reveals the next, conversational; answered steps collapse to editable summary chips; progress bar. The **engaged car carries in** as context ("Applying with …"). Persists to `obavia_application`.
- **Questions:** intent (multi: gig / daily driver / replacing a car in the shop / luxury occasion / just exploring — **NO ownership framing**, Graves Amendment + TX OCCC landmine) → name → soft gates: **age 18+**, valid license, insurance (have/willing), valid **non-prepaid** card, **Texas resident**, **driving record** (clean / minor=lenient pass / major DUI-reckless in 3 yrs = fail) → phone.
- **Result:** "**Pre-qualified · pending verification**" — NEVER "approved" (self-reported soft screen; verification is Stage C). Both: instant pre-qual + human review. Pre-qual **creates an Inquiry** in `obavia_leads` ({name, phone, purpose, timing, intent[], car, answers, prequalified, source:"application", status:"new"}) → flows into Admin Inquiries pipeline (Stage B accept/decline). **Graceful decline** = dignified off-ramp naming each specific blocker + exactly what would change it; "Keep me posted" writes a `status:"nurture"` lead. Honest-persuasion throughout (no fake urgency/scarcity, daylight test).
