# Obavia app

Before building here, read `../docs/obavia-spec.md` (positioning, feature spec with status, UX rules, design system). Background: `../docs/obavia-positioning.md`, `../docs/icp-language.md`, and the sales doctrine every coaching feature follows: `../docs/sales-doctrine.md`.

- One next action, one tap, nothing to type. Sound after first tap, haptics on taps only.
- No pills, chips, badges, emoji, disclaimer captions, em dashes, or text wordmark.
- The lead read is about language, never identity.
- Update the Status column in the spec when a feature ships.
